Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

This policy describes what Teslafield currently handles in the website and connected chat experience. It is intended to explain the actual product behaviour, not to promise that third-party providers follow the same practices.

1. Information used for sign-in

When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication verifies your identity. Teslafield uses your Firebase user ID to keep conversations separated between accounts. Teslafield does not need your Google password.

2. Conversation history

Chat history is cached in your browser's local storage under an account-specific key. The Stage 6 cloud-history feature can also sync conversations to the default Cloud Firestore database in the Teslafield Firebase project when the project owner has created that database and deployed the repository's owner-only security rules. Cloud sync is not a realtime listener; it synchronizes on sign-in, page focus and local changes. Until the database and rules are configured, conversations remain browser-local. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile may be able to access the local cache. It is not encrypted separately by Teslafield.

You can delete an individual conversation or clear saved history from the sidebar; when cloud history is enabled, those conversation deletions are also sent to Firestore. Clearing browser data may remove the local cache but does not delete the cloud copy.

Before you sign in, the first message you compose is temporarily kept in session storage in the current browser tab so Teslafield can send that same message after successful sign-in. The temporary draft is cleared after it is handed off or when the browser tab session ends.

3. AI processing by external providers

When you send a chat message, Teslafield sends the recent conversation context needed for the response to its backend over HTTPS. The backend forwards the request to the selected model provider: the TeslafieldAI Hugging Face chat Space by default, or OpenRouter when you explicitly select an OpenRouter model. OpenRouter free and paid model requests are subject to OpenRouter's own terms, privacy practices, retention and account limits. Paid model selection may incur charges from the user's OpenRouter account.

Voice input, microphone recording and read-aloud controls are not currently available in the Teslafield interface. The current website does not request microphone access or send microphone audio for processing.

These providers may process or retain requests according to their own terms, privacy notices and infrastructure settings. Teslafield cannot promise deletion from a provider's systems merely by deleting a conversation in your browser. Avoid sending passwords, payment card details or other highly sensitive information.

4. Files and audio

File selection currently performs browser-side file type, size and signature checks. Selected files remain on your device; file analysis and file upload through the Teslafield chat composer are not currently enabled.

Voice recording and speech transcription are available only after you sign in and grant microphone permission. Audio is sent to the backend for transcription; voice generation sends the text you choose to read aloud to the configured voice provider. Do not record another person without their permission.

5. Security logs

Teslafield's application security audit messages are designed not to record prompt text, conversation bodies, audio, file names, passwords or authentication tokens. They may record the event type, API route, timestamp and a shortened hash derived from the Firebase user ID. Hosting providers may also maintain infrastructure and network logs under their own policies.

6. Essential storage and cookie choices

Teslafield uses essential browser storage for sign-in state, security-related functionality, appearance preferences, locally saved conversations and your consent choices. Essential storage cannot be disabled without affecting core functionality.

Optional performance analytics and advertising/targeting trackers are not currently enabled by Teslafield. The cookie-choice dialog lets you save a preference for these categories. Accepting optional categories does not cause optional trackers to load while they are not installed.

You can reopen the consent choices at any time using “Your privacy choices” on the first-visit screen.

7. Account deletion

The account deletion control first attempts to delete known cloud conversation contents while the Firebase session is valid, then deletes the Firebase Authentication account and the local conversation cache for that account on the current browser. To prevent an older offline device from silently restoring a deleted conversation, minimal deletion-marker metadata (conversation ID and deletion timestamp) may remain in Firestore. These markers do not contain conversation messages. If cloud cleanup fails, account deletion is stopped and you are asked to retry. This process does not delete data held by Google, Hugging Face, OpenRouter or other external providers, or local browser copies on other devices.

8. Data minimisation

Teslafield aims to collect only information needed to authenticate requests, operate the interface and protect the API. We do not currently operate advertising or analytics trackers on the website.

9. Updates

This policy may be updated when Teslafield features or providers change. Check the effective date above for the current version.
